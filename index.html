Simulation settings

Automatic lossless disk cache · 1 GB maximum. Frequently reused frames retained; older unused frames evicted first. Keeps 64 MiB free. First loop computes every frame; replay is paced up to the selected FPS.

Generic material: n(λ)=n550+B(1/λ²−1/0.55²). B=0 disables chromatic bending. Suggested B=0.004 is illustrative, not measured for your plastic.

Drag numerical labels or use the wheel over a value; click the field to type. Shift gives fine steps, Ctrl coarse.

Material index and absorption are shared by all refracting layers. Source controls are on the Source card.

Physics & limitations

Pattern displays traced irradiance on the detector plane, including Snell refraction, Fresnel reflection, total internal reflection and absorption. Scene reuses it as a point projector with hard shadows; it does not trace room interreflection, near-field parallax or soft shadows.

Laser is a Gaussian geometric ray envelope, without coherent phase, diffraction, interference or speckle. The source and purchased collimator are not calibrated. Optical watts differ from the LED module’s electrical rating.
